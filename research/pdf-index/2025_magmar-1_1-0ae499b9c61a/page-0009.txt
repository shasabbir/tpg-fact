8 Ethics Statement
The VERITE dataset used in this study is publicly
available and specifically designed for benchmark-
ing multimodal fact-checking systems. All annota-
tion work was done by the study authors without
crowd-sourcing. VERITE consists of fact-checked
articles from Snopes and Reuters, curated by ex-
perts. Our pipeline retrieves only publicly available
evidence using official APIs of search engines that
comply with the Robots Exclusion Protocol.
