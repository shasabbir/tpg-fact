7 Conclusion
We have introduced a framework for temporal claim veri-
fication that incorporates temporal reasoning based on the
chronological order of events. By utilizing a multi-level at-
tention encoder, ChronoFact effectively captures the rel-
evance of evidence events to claim events. This enables
ChronoFact to accurately predict to claim event labels and
verify the chronological order consistency between claim and
evidence events before determining the overall claim label.
We have curated a new benchmark dataset that involves com-
plex claims with multiple events that may overlap or recur.
Extensive experiments on multiple datasets have shown that
ChronoFact outperforms state-of-the-art models.
