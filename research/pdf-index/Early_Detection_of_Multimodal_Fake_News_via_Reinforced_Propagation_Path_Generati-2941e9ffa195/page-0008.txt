D. Ablation Study
The ablation studies are conducted to investigate the impor-
tance of different modules in Table IV.
Heterogeneous Contrastive Learning: We aim to study the
inﬂuence of various contrastive learning methods. “w/o Node-
view CL” and “w/o Meta-path CL” stand for without node-view
contrastive learning, and meta-path contrastive learning. “w/o
all CL” stand for without all contrastive learning and initialize
with random user embedding. (1) The performance declines
with “w/o Node-view CL” or “w/o Meta-path CL”, revealing
that propagation information is as important as heterogeneous
relations for user embedding in social networks. (2) Without all
contrastive learning, performance declines signiﬁcantly, proving
both contrastive learning are crucial to generate the reasonable
user embedding.
