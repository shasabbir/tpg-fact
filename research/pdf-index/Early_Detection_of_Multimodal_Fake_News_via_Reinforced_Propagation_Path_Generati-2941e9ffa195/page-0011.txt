VI. C ONCLUSION
This paper introduces the innovative RPPG-Fake model. The
model simulates the propagation process using the contents
of news and integrates the generated propagation paths into
a temporal-aware detection module. The effectiveness of the
proposed model is thoroughly assessed across three popular
datasets. The experimental results demonstrate the enhanced
performance of our proposal. While the model shows promise,
future work could explore more sophisticated propagation path
generation techniques that account for additional social dy-
namics, incorporate adaptive learning for better generalization
across domains, and focus on optimizing real-time detection
and scalability for large-scale social networks. Addressing these
areas will be essential to maximizing the model’s impact and
applicability across diverse contexts.
