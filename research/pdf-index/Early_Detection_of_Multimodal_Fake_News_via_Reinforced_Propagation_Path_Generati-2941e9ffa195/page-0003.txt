A. Heterogeneous Graph Contrastive Learning
Previous propagation enhanced models [16], [17] often focus
on incorporating individual news propagation paths as sup-
plementary information to identify fake news. However, these
methods are limited in capturing the global propagation features
across all news on social platforms. The dissemination of news
on social platforms involves various roles (such as publishers,
users, and news content) that are interconnected in diverse
relationships, offering valuable insights for understanding the
intricate propagation interactions. To comprehensively model
the spreading of news on social platforms, we have constructed
a heterogeneous graph denoted as H =( V,E,U), where V
represents the node set, E is the edge set with edges belonging
toV×V , and U is the embedding feature set. Due to the social
heterogeneous graph containing a large number of unlabeled
interaction data, we introduce a dual-view self-supervised con-
trastive mechanism to learn the topology structure feature and
heterogeneous relationship feature.
1) Node View Contrastive Learning: To capture the global
topology structure feature across social platforms effectively,
we propose a contrastive learning mechanism based on node
view. The ﬁrst step is to generate positive and negative node
pairs for every node in the graph H. Inspired by PGD algo-
rithm [18], we introduce a perturbation δ into node features to
create more challenging instances for both positive and negative
pairs in contrastive learning. The perturbed user node feature
is denoted as ~u = u+δ. Then positive and negative node sets
are constructed. For each node vi ∈V , two sets of nodes, Vpi
and Vni , are deﬁned to contain the top K1 highest and lowest
cosine similarities with the center node vi [19]. Subsequently,
the Graph Attention Network (GA T) encoder [20] is utilized to
obtain the node embedding:
