On two public datasets, Twitter and Weibo, our proposed MDF-
FND consistently outperforms the latest baseline methods. The 4% im-
provement in accuracy over the optimal baseline model was achieved. 
Correspondingly, the F1 Score increases by 6%. In this paper, the data 
uncertainty problem caused by the noise in multimodal posts circulat-
ing in social networks is elaborated, while the uncertainty modeling 
mechanism is introduced in our model, which is able to capture the 
unimodal features in complex heterogeneous multimodal post data. In 
addition, the heterogeneous GAT shifts the goal to modeling the inter-
modal uncertainty, and the mapped features have stronger robustness; 
thus the performance is effectively improved. However, because the 
two datasets originate from distinct linguistic environments, tweets 
on the Twitter platform are typically brief, while Weibo posts often 
include trending topic tags and a broader range of content expressions. 
Consequently, there are substantial differences between the results.
The MDF-FND framework exhibits poor performance on the Poli-
tiFact dataset, and our analysis reveals two primary reasons for this. 
First, the PolitiFact dataset relies on factual verification, where external 
knowledge is crucial for guiding detection. Thus, relying solely on 
semantic information is inadequate to accurately identify fake news. 
Second, the dataset contains numerous posts without accompanying 
images, and the MDF-FND framework struggles to process these posts 
effectively. If a multimodal post lacks information from one modality, 
the dynamic fusion approach is divided into traditional unimodal fake 
detection tasks, which presents a considerable challenge for the model. 
In the following experiments, the overall performance of the MDF-FND 
framework is mainly evaluated on the two public datasets, Twitter and 
Weibo.
In summary, the advantage of the MDF-FND framework lies in 
its ability to model both intra-modal and inter-modal uncertainty by 
utilizing the strengths of the attention mechanism, thus improving the 
robustness of the detection model. However, its limitation is that, in the 
context of fact-checking news, it cannot incorporate additional consid-
erations, particularly clues relevant to fact verification. This represents 
a promising direction for future research.

4.6. Ablation studies
To verify the effectiveness of the proposed fusion strategy, the abla-
tion analyses are conducted to evaluate the impact of each component 
on the performance of the MDF-FND framework. In each experiment, a 
specific component, either the UEM or DFN, is systematically removed 
and the model is retrained. The following variants of MDF-FND are 
implemented:
1. MDF-FND w/o UEM-Text. The uncertainty module used to model 
unimodal text is removed. The UEM module features are fed with 
captured visual uncertainty information and features extracted 
after BERT [46] into the dynamic fusion module, and Gaussian 
modeling for text features is not removed to better represent the 
modeling capabilities of DFN.
