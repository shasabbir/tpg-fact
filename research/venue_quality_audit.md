## Priority tiers used in the study

1. **Tier 1:** 2024--2025 A* conference papers and 2025 Q1 journal papers closest to the task.
2. **Tier 2:** recent A conference papers and older A* papers that define datasets or evaluation standards.
3. **Tier 3:** workshops, Findings papers, and related studies useful for baselines, implementation details, or failure analysis.

Venue tier never overrides a direct methodological contradiction or a stronger experimental control in a lower-tier paper.
