# Remaining publication actions

1. Authors choose the target venue and confirm whether the contribution will be
   submitted as a bounded development/measurement study or strengthened by an
   external confirmation run.
2. If external confirmation is chosen, freeze one compatible evidence unit,
   split, metric set, calibration rule, and stopping rule before seeing its
   test outcomes. Do not reuse MOCHEG calibration parameters.
3. Authors supply factual declaration text: CRediT roles, funding, conflicts,
   acknowledgments, ethics applicability, and exact data/code availability.
4. Convert the manuscript to the selected venue's page limit and anonymity
   requirements; move extended robustness and audit tables to a supplement if
   required.
5. Build the final source archive in a clean directory and repeat the PDF,
   reference, and test checks.
6. Keep the formal MOCHEG test sealed unless the authors explicitly authorize
   the frozen one-time evaluation.
