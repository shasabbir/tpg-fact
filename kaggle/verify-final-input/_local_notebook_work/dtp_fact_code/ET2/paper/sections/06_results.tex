\section{Results}

The real-data feasibility pilot and frozen model runs have not yet been completed. Consequently, this draft reports no accuracy, calibration, retrieval, or efficiency result, and it makes no superiority claim. Synthetic smoke-test outputs are software diagnostics and are excluded from the manuscript's scientific evidence.

The completed study will report three result blocks generated directly from preserved artifacts. The first covers corpus yield, exclusions, timestamp confidence, label balance, sufficiency-audit agreement, and dependency-cluster quality. The second covers retrieval, three-class prediction, calibration, and selective risk for the matched baselines. The third reports every counterfactual intervention, ablation, topic slice, and chronological window, including negative or inconclusive findings. The 2025 and partial-2026 results remain separate in addition to the combined test result.
