\section{Conclusion}

This study defines a test of decision-time provenance in multimodal fact-checking. The proposed protocol requires independently observed pre-cutoff evidence, limits the influence of dependent copies, separates evidence sufficiency from selective deferral, and evaluates these choices on future events through reconstructable interventions. The contribution will stand only if the feasibility gate is met and the corresponding comparisons support it. Until those experiments are complete, \systemname{} remains an implemented research protocol rather than a validated fact-checking system.
