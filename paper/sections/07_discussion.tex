\section{Discussion, Ethics, and Limitations}

The principal methodological risk is mistaking accessible web redundancy for independent corroboration. The proposed dependency clusters are estimates based on observable content and links; they do not prove journalistic independence or original authorship. Similarly, archived timestamps indicate observed availability and may differ from the true first publication time.

The corpus inherits professional fact-check labels and their editorial definitions. Restriction to explicit rating mappings reduces ambiguity but can create selection bias and class imbalance. United States events and English sources limit geographic and linguistic generalization. Common Crawl and GDELT coverage is incomplete, and removed or blocked content may be systematically different from recoverable content.

No copyrighted full text or images will be redistributed without permission. The release will emphasize URLs, hashes, derived metadata, split manifests, and collection code. The system is intended to support research and human review, not to serve as an autonomous censorship or content-removal mechanism. A prediction of insufficient evidence should trigger further investigation rather than imply that a claim is false.
