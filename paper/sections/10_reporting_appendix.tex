\subsection{Reproducibility Record}

A repository README specifies the frozen configurations, result directories,
and reporting bundles. The earlier diagnostic clean rerun places outputs in a
separate directory; all 20 core selector and verifier artifacts match
byte-for-byte by SHA-256. The bounded repair adds source hashes for retrieval,
selector, completeness, and verifier summaries and independent audits for each
stage. Runtime-bearing summaries are independently recomputed rather than
expected to match byte-for-byte.

There are exactly 1,490 locked-validation claim records in each of the five
selector partitions for each method. The repaired candidate bundle contains
789,540 rows for 13,159 released train and validation claims before the
complete-gold eligibility filter. Candidate records store scorer output,
calibrated probability, rank, modality, gold membership, and selected-set
decisions. Selector, completeness, verifier, audit, and reporting records all
contain false formal-test access flags.

The external preparation retains 94,085 training candidates and 18,240
development candidates after ranking the complete organizer stores. The
training freeze, development pre-join ranking, locked claim records, and
independently recomputed report have SHA-256 identifiers beginning
\texttt{36e4210a}, \texttt{b24739c9}, \texttt{eec62a20}, and
\texttt{0751ce76}, respectively. The full identifiers and archive hashes are
preserved with the result bundle. Local fixed-policy development exports for
all five seeds contain 152 predictions, satisfy the official schema, and were
not submitted.

\subsection{External Evaluation Path}
\label{sec:external}

AVerImaTeC provides a static multimodal knowledge store and a four-class fact-
checking task \cite{cao2026averimatecshared}. The completed transfer protocol
maps each source and 1,500-character text chunk to deterministic identifiers,
combines BM25 and BGE-M3 ranks by reciprocal-rank fusion, applies a frozen BGE
reranker, and ranks actual images with SigLIP2 and filename-BGE scores. It fits
every scorer, calibrator, completeness model, and verifier from scratch on the
793 released training claims. Gold questions are not retrieval features, and
gold-source membership is joined only after label-blind rankings are written
and hashed.

The organizer archives contain 529,580 text and 81,817 image candidates for
training and 101,289 text and 49,617 image candidates for development. The
five-seed training protocol was committed before development preparation. A
single locked run then evaluated all 152 development claims. The retained
100-text/20-image pool reaches 25.48\% of exact annotated sources, fully
reaches 12.50\% of claims, and reaches none for 59.87\% of claims. Exact-source
Recall@10, @20, @50, and @100 is 5.26\%, 6.96\%, 11.43\%, and 23.78\%.

Only the calibration comparison passes: calibrated expected-F1 exceeds its
uncalibrated counterpart by 0.05316 with simultaneous interval
$[0.02207,0.08852]$. Thresholding is tied with fixed selection. Expected-F1
selects 3.20 rather than 1.17 items, so the compactness claim does not transfer.
The switch interval crosses zero, returns a larger set, and fails the verifier
safeguard. The frozen decision therefore falls back to the train-selected
fixed family.

The prescribed Gemma-3-27B judge contains 54.86~GB of weights, whereas the
available RTX~4090 has 25.76~GB of memory and the organizer launcher requests
two GPUs. We do not call the exact-source diagnostic an official task score or
substitute a quantized judge. The blind test and leaderboard remain untouched.
The completed external study is consequently a prespecified diagnostic
nonreplication: calibration dependence transfers, while selector superiority,
compact expected-F1 selection, and verifier preservation do not.
