\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Frozen Protocols and Claim Boundary}

The diagnostic configuration was fixed on 4 November 2026 before its
outcome-bearing run. It records data and model revisions, five claim-group
partition seeds, retrieval depths, scorer families, calibration candidates,
selector grids, evaluation metrics, bootstrap settings, and stopping rules.
The bounded retrieval repair, completeness-aware switch, and stronger verifier
were frozen on 9 November 2026 before their respective outcomes were inspected.
The numeric threshold grid and verifier architecture were recorded before the
affected models were fitted. All amendments and failed runs remain in an
append-only experiment ledger.

The external implementation was committed before development preparation, and
the five-seed train-only manifest was committed on 11 November 2026. That
manifest records code, configuration, archive, pre-join ranking, candidate,
gold-map, and audit hashes and rejects a mixed train--development input. The
152-claim development matrix was then executed once on 12 November 2026.

The released MOCHEG validation split had been used earlier in the project and
is therefore controlled development evidence, not fresh confirmation. The
formal-test annotations are absent from the execution path and remain sealed.
No validation outcome automatically authorizes a formal-test run.

\subsection{Independent External Protocol}

AVerImaTeC training claims are deterministically partitioned for seeds 13, 37,
73, 101, and 137 into 55\% fit, 15\% calibration, 15\% model selection, and
15\% internal holdout roles. The locked development run compares train-selected
fixed top-$k$, calibrated thresholding, calibrated expected-F1, uncalibrated
expected-F1, and the completeness-aware switch on the same candidates and
membership scores. No development result changes retrieval, fitting,
calibration, packet width, or policy parameters.

Four claim-aligned comparisons form one multiplicity family: threshold minus
fixed, expected-F1 minus threshold, calibrated minus uncalibrated expected-F1,
and switch minus threshold. Each uses 10,000 bootstrap draws, Bonferroni
simultaneous intervals, and Holm-adjusted two-sided values. Threshold
superiority requires an effect of at least $+0.010$ and a positive adjusted
lower bound. Expected-F1 must clear a $-0.010$ non-inferiority margin and return
at least 15\% fewer items. Switch superiority additionally requires every seed
effect to be positive and no increase in mean set size. Verifier preservation
uses the same $-0.010$ margin.

The organizer's semantic evaluator uses Gemma-3-27B in bfloat16. Its 54.86~GB
of weight files do not fit the single 24.56~GiB RTX~4090 under the prescribed
condition, and the organizer launcher specifies two GPUs. We therefore report
exact normalized-source diagnostics and class-wise verifier results, not a
replacement score labeled as official. The blind test remains outside the
authorized execution boundary.

\subsection{Baselines, Outcomes, and Selection Budgets}

The matched policies are fixed top-5, calibrated thresholding, learned count,
calibrated expected-F1, uncalibrated expected-F1, and the
packet-completeness-aware switch. The primary outcome is claim-macro
complete-gold evidence F1. Precision, recall, Jaccard similarity, exact-set
match, selected count, calibration metrics, reachability, and verdict macro-F1
are secondary outcomes.

Scorer and calibration selection uses training roles only. The repair examines
three declared weights per retrieval branch. Thresholding searches
$\{0.1,\ldots,0.9\}$, and the completeness cutoff searches five declared
values. No additional retrieval branch is opened because the weighted-fusion
advancement gate passes. These budgets prevent outcome-driven expansion of the
search after locked validation is observed.

For each comparison, claim-level differences are first averaged within claim
over the five deterministic partitions. A claim-aligned bootstrap then
resamples the 1,490 claims 10,000 times. The selector-superiority gate requires
all of the following against calibrated thresholding: at least $+0.010$
evidence F1, a positive paired 95\% interval after multiplicity control,
positive effects in all five partitions, and no increase in mean selected-set
size. The verifier uses a $-0.010$ non-inferiority margin. We report both the
aggregate seed-aligned interval and the stricter requirement that every
seed-specific lower bound clear the margin.

\subsection{Robustness and Diagnostic Analyses}

The original diagnostic packet is also evaluated under widths 10 and 20,
text-only and image-only evidence, deterministic removal of 25\% and 50\% of
reachable gold, promotion of five distractors, duplication of the top three
items, and probability temperatures of 0.5 and 2.0. Fitted selectors do not
change under these perturbations. The conditions test dependence on
reachability, modality, ranking contamination, duplication, and probability
scale; they are sensitivity analyses rather than estimates of production
shift.

Following the primary comparison, validation claims are divided into no,
partial, or complete released-gold reachability. This stratification is post
hoc and descriptive. It motivates the prespecified completeness-aware switch
but does not turn the earlier subgroup result into a confirmatory claim.

\subsection{Implementation, Hardware, and Audit}

Every run stores candidate-level scores, calibrated probabilities, selected
identifiers, claim-level metrics, fitted parameters, environment metadata, and
formal-test access flags. Independent audits recompute population counts, set
metrics, paired effects, and gate conditions from saved records. Label-blind
rankings are hashed before gold is joined. Partial outputs from four failed
runs are preserved with the repair reason rather than overwritten.

The weighted retrieval run takes 9.5 seconds, candidate construction 8.4
seconds, base selector evaluation 158.8 seconds, completeness switching 334.8
seconds, and the stronger verifier 246.9 seconds on this machine. These are
wall-clock records for reproducibility, not cross-system efficiency results.
The faithful organizer-store preparation takes 50.5 minutes for 793 training
claims and 21.2 minutes for 152 development claims; the locked five-seed
comparison takes 19 seconds after preparation. These times include text
encoding, cross-encoder reranking, and actual-image SigLIP2 scoring. One
144-million-pixel development image triggers a PIL safety warning but is
processed without exclusion, OOM, or fallback.
