\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Controlled Comparisons}

The majority baseline predicts the training-set majority label and emits no citation. Claim-only removes the evidence path. All-pool mean-pools every valid candidate. Relevance ranks candidates by scaled claim--evidence dot product. Flat uses the learned selector without graph layers. Graph-fixed uses the full graph selector and emits its top three items; this is the matched verifier from which \method{} is derived. Fixed top-one and top-two outputs cut the same Graph-fixed ranking without retraining. \method{} uses that identical ranking but predicts a claim-specific count with its separately trained cardinality head.

M-RAV, MEVER, and MOCHEG are discussed as published context \cite{luu2027mrav,zhang2026mever,yao2023mocheg}; their published values are not entered into significance tests because they use different datasets, candidate pools, and label spaces.

\subsection{Evaluation Measures}

The co-primary endpoints are verdict macro-F1 and claim-level citation macro-F1. Citation precision, recall, and F1 are computed from predicted and gold evidence-ID sets for each claim and then averaged. The main method comparison is \method{} versus its matched Graph-fixed top-three output. Exact equality of their verdict probabilities and predictions is a mandatory integrity condition.

Secondary verdict measures are accuracy, balanced accuracy, per-class precision, recall and F1, multiclass Brier score, negative log-likelihood, and 10-bin expected calibration error. Ranking quality is measured by MAP@10, Recall@10, and nDCG@10. Citation-cardinality measures are mean absolute count error, exact count match, mean predicted count, and mean gold count. Citation precision, recall, and F1 are stratified by gold evidence count and by text-only versus visually grounded claims. Applying the gold count to the frozen ranking is reported only as an oracle-cardinality diagnostic, not as a deployable baseline.

Runtime, throughput, peak training GPU memory, and checkpoint size document computation. The existing channel-removal and perturbation outputs are retained as diagnostics for the base verifier but are not contributions or success criteria for \method{}.

\subsection{Model Selection and Statistical Analysis}

Base checkpoints maximize the unweighted mean of development verdict and citation macro-F1. With the base checkpoint frozen, the cardinality head is selected by development citation macro-F1. Neither selection step uses the official test. The formal experiment runs all learned configurations with five predetermined seeds and identical feature stores within a seed.

Uncertainty is estimated with 10,000 paired bootstrap samples over source-paper groups, preserving every claim from a sampled paper. For each resample, the paired metric difference is computed within each shared seed and averaged across seeds. The analysis reports the mean difference, percentile 95\% confidence interval, and two-sided bootstrap $p$ value. An exact seed-level sign-flip test is included as a descriptive stability check; with five seeds, its resolution is coarse. Holm adjustment is applied separately to the prespecified verdict and citation comparison families.

The adaptive-sizing improvement claim requires a positive official-test citation effect for \method{} relative to each prespecified fixed top-one, top-two, and top-three rule, with all three grouped-bootstrap intervals reported and Holm correction applied to this comparison family, while every paired verdict output remains identical. If any comparison is non-positive, no adaptive superiority claim is made. Subgroup claims are limited to strata with adequate support and are labeled exploratory otherwise.

\subsection{Ablations and Diagnostic Comparisons}

All-pool, relevance, flat, and Graph-fixed isolate evidence use, learned ranking, and graph contextualization. Fixed top-one, top-two, and top-three isolate the best global citation budget. The joint adaptive model, evaluated only during development, is retained as a negative design diagnostic because it changes the verifier representation; it is not part of the official-test comparison family. For the decoupled head, removing the valid-candidate count or visual-availability count tests whether those packet summaries add value beyond sorted selector scores. Gold-cardinality ranking performance estimates how much remaining error belongs to ordering rather than set size.

\subsection{Reproducibility Controls}

Dataset revisions, raw and processed hashes, split manifests, feature hashes, configuration, fingerprint-bound checkpoints, predictions, complete frozen rankings, gold/prediction comparison records, confidence intervals, training histories, code hashes, artifact inventories, and environment metadata are stored with each run. The release gate rejects source-paper overlap, exact visual overlap, or exact gold-evidence content overlap across splits. The finalization gate rejects missing seeds or configurations, non-finite metrics, incomplete prediction files, checkpoint-input mismatches, altered DACS rankings, or any difference between DACS and matched verdict records. Smoke and development-pilot outputs cannot be promoted into the manuscript.
