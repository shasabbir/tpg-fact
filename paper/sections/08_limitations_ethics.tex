\section{Limitations and Ethical Considerations}

The present manuscript is a pre-results protocol. The real semantic selector, full-corpus retrieval, shared verifier, five-seed formal matrix, robustness tests, and external validation are not yet complete. The 400-claim feasibility packet starts from released judgment candidates, so it can overestimate performance relative to open-corpus retrieval. Expected-F1 decoding assumes conditionally independent evidence-membership variables even though documents and duplicated images may be dependent. Temperature scaling cannot correct a poorly ranked candidate pool. MOCHEG contains inherited professional fact-check labels and English-language web evidence; results may not transfer to other languages, domains, or live information environments. Public mirrors reproduce official counts but remain secondary transports, and missing grouping metadata may force claim-level rather than event-level uncertainty estimates. These constraints prevent any current claim of deployment readiness or general fact-checking reliability.

The system is intended to assist evidence review, not autonomously determine truth. Incorrect verdicts or incomplete evidence can misrepresent people, events, and sources. The project uses public research data, records provenance and immutable identifiers, and does not redistribute credentials or private user data. Generated explanations are excluded from the primary claim because fluent text is not evidence of correct attribution. Formal outputs must preserve probabilities, selected identifiers, hashes, and failure records so that unfavorable results remain auditable.
