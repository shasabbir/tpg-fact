\section{Limitations and Ethics}
\label{sec:limitations}

\subsection{Limitations}

For one, the reported study on selectors is based on MOCHEG validation claims which have been published. This run was locked, but previous analysis conducted as part of this overall project had utilized the same development resource. The outcome is a controlled reanalysis; new external confirmation is still pending. The official MOCHEG test annotations are kept sealed.

Second, complete-gold annotations may be incomplete in terms of all valid web evidence. Exact-set metrics reward agreement with the given mapping and may label a reasonable alternative as incorrect. The reachability-stratified analysis is post hoc and the complete-reachability result is based on 79 validation claims. It is a mechanism diagnosis which necessitates prespecified replication and cannot offer a population-level subgroup guarantee.

Thirdly, the membership scorers are designed to be as lightweight as possible to maintain experimental control and reproducibility. They are not intended as state-of-the-art representation learners. Better multimodal rerankers could change both ranking and calibration. Another corpus should be used to test the repeated selection of isotonic regression for images.

The saved records can determine the software environment and the runtime of the deterministic analysis, but do not reveal a complete end-to-end analysis of embedding and retrieval in terms of compute, latency or energy. Thus, no efficiency claim is made in the study other than the number of items of evidence returned to a reviewer.

Fourth, the expected-F1 calculation is based on the assumption that there is conditional independence of the candidate memberships. The evidence items within the same source, duplicate images and complementary sentences are in violation of this assumption. The tested redundancy penalty wasn't of assistance, though, which doesn't preclude a learnt structured model.

Fifth, the robustness conditions are diagnostics and only address a small portion of possible production drift. The temperature perturbations do not estimate the frequency of such distortion in a deployed system; they only measure sensitivity to probability scale.

Lastly, external datasets are actually different. AVerImaTeC involves web or curated question-answer evidence and MMCV involves tables and multi-hop support. Porting the selector involves a redefinition of evidence units, a new calibration study and an evaluation done with sensitivity to the access conditions of each resource.

\subsection{Ethical Considerations}

Automated fact-checking can also engender a false sense of security, especially when evidence is not retrieved. The interface for any deployment should include identifiers for the sources, restrictions on the retrievals, probability calibration status, and uncertainty. The system should support trained reviewers, not replace them.

Geographic, language and source-selection biases can be carried over to data and model artefacts. MOCHEG is not meant to be a representative sample of all claims or communities. Generalisation of performance to low-resource languages or sensitive domains should be avoided unless specifically evaluated. Personal, disturbing or copyrighted material may be included in web evidence, and access and display should be in keeping with the terms of the source and minimize unnecessary reproduction.

The formal test is still kept sealed, bad and unfavourable results are kept and all comparisons reported are accompanied by uncertainty. These controls minimize the selective reporting and it is easier to differentiate a helpful development finding from a deployment claim.
