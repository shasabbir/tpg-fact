\section{Limitations and Ethical Considerations}

Public benchmarks can appear in foundation-model pretraining data, so test performance does not prove contamination-free generalization. Scientific datasets are concentrated in English and in a limited set of fields. Their refuted claims may be constructed through controlled perturbations rather than naturally occurring scientific misconduct. MOCHEG contains web-derived media whose continued availability and usage terms require auditing.

Gold evidence is not a complete representation of every valid proof. Citation precision can penalize an uncatalogued but correct evidence item, while citation recall can reward evidence that is listed but not necessary. Counterfactual interventions approximate deployment failures and may introduce artifacts. These limitations require example-level error analysis and conservative conclusions.

The system is intended to assist evidence review, not to adjudicate scientific truth without human oversight. Incorrect verdicts or persuasive but incomplete explanations can harm authors and readers. Released predictions must retain dataset identifiers, uncertainty, and provenance. Source media are not redistributed when licenses do not permit it, and no private user data are collected.
