\section{Limitations and Ethical Considerations}

This manuscript reports a completed development decision, not a formal-test result. The 400-claim feasibility packet begins with released judgment candidates and clearly overestimates performance relative to the label-blind full-corpus comparison. There are 367 eligible claims in the development sample after the fixed exclusion policy, rather than all released train/development claims. Conditionally independent evidence-membership variables are assumed even though documents and duplicated images may be dependent. A poorly ranked group of candidates cannot be corrected by temperature scaling, and the simple logistic scorer is not a capacity measure. Since the primary development gate failed, robustness tests, the five-seed formal matrix and external validation were not performed. MOCHEG includes inherited professional fact-check labels and English-language web evidence, so results may not be comparable to other languages, domains or live information environments. Public mirrors reproduce the official counts but remain secondary transports; claim-level uncertainty is used because reliable event grouping is unavailable. All these limitations preclude any assertion of deployment readiness or a general claim to fact-checking reliability.

The system is designed to aid in evidence review, not to automatically decide on the truth. Verdicts or evidence may be wrong or incomplete and result in misrepresentation of people, events and sources. The project leverages public research information, records provenance and immutable identifiers but does not pass on credentials or private user information. Fluently written explanations do not count as evidence of proper attribution and are therefore not included in the main claim. Failure results, selected identifiers, hashes, and probabilities must be retained as formal outputs to allow the unfavorable results to be auditable.
