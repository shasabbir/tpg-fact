\section{Limitations and Ethical Considerations}

The evidence is limited to one English scientific benchmark, whose refuted claims may be controlled perturbations rather than naturally occurring misinformation. Public claims may also have appeared in encoder pretraining data. The 12-item within-paper packets simplify open-web retrieval, and frozen BGE/CLIP features can miss numerical or visual details. Gold evidence is incomplete: citation metrics may penalize an uncatalogued valid proof or reward redundant annotated items. Ten low-Hamming-distance visual pairs remain across splits, although none shares an exact raw or corrected perceptual hash and their serialized content differs. The cardinality head cannot abstain, is unstable across seeds, and was selected on only 196 validation claims. MuSciClaims was excluded because authoritative release-license metadata was unavailable. Consequently, the findings concern metric alignment under a frozen SciVer ranking and do not establish cross-domain fact-checking performance, calibrated confidence, or causal evidence use.

The system is intended for evidence-review research, not autonomous adjudication of scientific truth. Incorrect verdicts or incomplete citations can misrepresent prior work. Released outputs should preserve identifiers, probabilities, citations, and provenance; this study collects no private user data and does not redistribute restricted source media.
